About Painting Cabinet

A free educational viewer for paintings from around the world. It exists to help people wander, learn and discover artists they might never have searched for.

Why it exists

Great paintings are scattered across countless museum websites and archives, and it can be hard to know what to look for. Painting Cabinet gathers freely available works into one quiet place, so you can compare schools and eras, follow an artist's career, or simply let the slideshow surprise you.

Ways to explore

The Viewer A slow slideshow of paintings. Narrow it by school, genre and year, and click any painting to zoom in close. Open the viewer → The Artists Choose a school, pick a painter, and see every work in the cabinet by them, with a link to read more. Browse artists →

Where the information comes from

Everything is built from open public sources.

  • Artist listsWikipedia
  • Facts about artists and worksWikidata — titles, dates and genres
  • PicturesWikimedia Commons — public domain or freely licensed files

Famous artists have many works here, and lesser-known artists may have only a few or none at all. That gap is part of the record, not something we hide.

Good to know

  • It is not a complete catalogue of every painting an artist made.
  • Dates and genres come from open data and are partly assigned automatically, so some are missing, approximate or wrong.
  • It is not a museum, and it is not affiliated with Wikipedia, Wikimedia or any museum.
  • Not every listed painter has pictures here yet. The collection is still growing.
