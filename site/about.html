About

Painting Cabinet is a free educational website for looking at paintings by Flemish, Dutch, and French artists. It is meant to help people discover artists and works, not to sell art.

What this site is

A viewer built from open public sources. You will be able to browse by school (Flemish, Dutch, French), then later by artist, year, and genre.

What this site is not

  • Not a complete catalogue of every painting those artists made.
  • Not a museum, and not affiliated with Wikipedia or any museum.
  • Not a place that claims every listed painter has images here.

Where the information will come from

  • Artist lists: Wikipedia
  • Facts about artists and works: Wikidata
  • Pictures: Wikimedia Commons (public domain or freely licensed files)

Famous artists will have many works. Lesser-known artists may have few or none. That gap is part of the record, not a bug we hide.

Status

The catalog is not built yet. These pages are the start of the public site.
